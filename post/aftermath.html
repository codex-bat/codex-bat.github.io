I broke the sink and it was leaking for hours while I was apparently sleeping (thank gosh my body knows how to keep itself alive... I'm so thankful... and so annoyed at myself).

I remember recording fortnite in case I got the token and apparently that video is 22GBs long now, so it might give me a clue as to what happened while I was full drunk.

anywho! byebye now... please take care of yourself and never drink this much... please please please, I beg of you. I'm really sorry I did this... I hope I'm not disappointing you. why did I do this to myself!?

November 24, the fallout & revival: ok, what the f*ck am I doing with these goofy titles? here's the tea: this is a post that was sitting in my drafts basically, while I was recovering. I don't exactly remember everything I've said about the blackout in the moment. I was basically sleeping through the first few days trying to calm my breathing, knowing I had nothing better I could do as I was unable to leave the house. I was horrified at what happened. I even had some mood swings. I broke down crying while talking to redoubt once or twice. it was really hard on me what I had experienced and the fact that I had experienced it alone. on day two after my incident, I had decided I wanted to go to the ER to see how I am and what happened to me, but for that I thought I should check out the footage I had of me playing and such. it's corrupt and I had to use old player software to be able to navigate through it. I remember mildly ranting about "being a responsible drinker", as I "have no one around me I could hurt"... I'd ask "what have I been drinking" but we know already. this part really... really shook me to my core though. I was saying "I don't have the time, money, knowledge or resources for a lawsuit", but I'll "win it anyway" or something something. what a mess... what a mess... I have no proof to know any of this for sure, but the faint image seems like a memory. it's not the only one I have and there's one or two similarly faint ones I am sure of.

well, no matter... I went through it. I pushed through the nights with little to no sleep and slept out of exhaustion during the day. I was really stressed. I kept analysing my symptoms to no avail, because I had little knowledge on what to worry about. my body generally started hurting on days 1-3 in bed, but nothing major. my chest and throat hurt a lot though, and I was hoping to associate it with PTSD-like anxiety, but I couldn't rule out aspiration issues and it was really stressing me out. on the 22nd of November, I had decided to finally go to the ER and tell them everything.

the one-liner was "I had lost consciousness 3-4 days ago from drinking". at the reception they asked me "from what? from running??", no darling... from drinking. I'd have to assume they were giving the chance to correct my narrative for some reason, but in the moment I hadn't picked up on it. there was another couple there trying to get treatment as well. I was let in first and after I had initially told the doctor my one-liner and the symptom of "trying to gasp for air"/"not enough air", there was silence until he finally told me to get my personal details out. the attitude wasn't the same with the couple though, the doctor seemed to be a bit more vocal with them. I get that drinking isn't quite a great look... but surely you don't look at me being a miserable-looking guy, with one hand having black-painted nails and deduce that I'm a waste, right? well... he asked if me EU healthcare card had another side as well photographed, but I checked and playwright hadn't sent me that. I told them I could ask for it if they needed it. I was trying to be cooperative, but the doctor just told me "I'll write something" and that's it...

it took a long time for them to get me in. I heard the dude move things around. I now realize it wasn't them not being prepared, it was them not being prepared for me --- you'll see why ---, because the couple was told to come in right after me, no wait... no dilly-dallying. I hate how I always seem to find myself in situations where I'm treated differently for whatever reason. anyway, when I got in, he began checking the basic stuff. I was feeling lightheaded most of the time --- even getting to the ER was a puzzle I had to figure out with how to make it as efficient to my efforts as possible. I started telling him: I had a blackout... I vomitted a lot... I mean the consequences suggest so, anyway. I also think I fell as I found a bruise above my elbow (right one), etc. no reaction at all whatsoever. a second nurse/doctor came in and at first I thought it was a typical check-up on something between them. happens sometimes. but once she sat down I realized this is probably a social worker and I had already realized how conveniently strategic everything was. it bothers me how I naively didn't realize anything sooner or how I didn't try and stand up for myself, because I was at a really low point and I was really battling with my general body's light sensation in the moment. apparently my temperature was 37.9 --- not a fever by strict thresholds, but really close. I have to assume it's just skin-layer temperature and not alarming, but my pulse was borderline and there is little excuse to that. I wasn't informed of this. my blood pressure was 123 though, that was fine. they asked me if I had a fever and I told them I didn't have knowledge of having one.

and that's when this social worker lady started drilling me; asked me why I drank. asked if it was a challenge. asked who I drank with. asked how often I drink, but they never asked non-pointed questions. it wasn't a "getting to know me" and at least of that I was aware of in the moment. I realized she wasn't my friend, but I thought she was just doing her job. however, her questions really weren't actually doing any job other than drilling and drilling. instead of asking how often I drink, it was "how often do you reach for alcohol as a way to deal with things/ease the pain or to lose control" and such. she then asked why, though leading. asked if I was struggling. they were trying to build a psychological profile on me, it was annoying. I laughed a few times, but I told them the truth entirely, because I really didn't see a big deal. not anymore. it was traumatic, but it wasn't reoccuring. I know my posts have been building to this, but surely it's seen clearly that I'm not an alcoholic. I don't drink like an alcoholic, especially because my threshold is way lower.

so let's see what I answered... I told them this hasn't happened like this before. I rarely drink on my own. I mostly drank with draupner wave, because I knew it's how she could spend time with me. I also told them she'd want to drink basically almost daily and I'd refuse most of the time. I told them I very rarely drink on my own out of my own accord and mostly just to feel "a bit more fun, a bit more party-like". my choice of words wasn't exactly the best, I don't want to feel "party-like", just a bit funnier. she asked if it helps me be less burdened, but none of this was asked like they were talking to an equal or a human being really. they were talking to me like I was a lowlife (not saying lowlifes aren't human beings, it's how they're often treated). they treated me like I have been drinking for years to solve all of my problems and as if I was an irresponsible nobody with nothing to give. it was very demeaning. I still answered their questions honestly and I still couldn't help but crack defeated smiles when the questions were pointed very obviously in certain directions. I don't like that I smiled, because I didn't feel identified... maybe my body disagrees, but I genuinely don't thinkg I'm an alcoholic. am I delusional for thinking... no, knowing that?

there was only one question where I started hesitating to answer with full honesty, because I realized how it'll sound. they asked me if I'm often struggling or whatever. I said "well not really, but I mean a bit I guess". yeah, I've been struggling a lot! a lot of system lockout situations and all of the hassle that comes with it, but none of that pointed me in the direction of alcohol. the single thing I said while ranting angrily drunk in that footage that I genuinely know is true is that I said in it that "nobody gives a shit. nobody gave a shit if I died right now. nobody cares if I drink too much. nobody cares!! NOBODYY CARES!!!" and while that often hurts and it hurts that I think that, it's still not reason why I consciously would choose to drink a lot. as I said in sage, I wanted to "forget a bit". sure, I wanted to see myself have less surgical control over my body and while alcohol often helped me ease some of the bodily tensions I was having in the past, I still didn't ever see alcohol as my friend. I had a love-hate relationship with alcohol represented in shows. take hazbin hotel for example, I completely understand why someone would love a person whose "soulmate is alcohol" and I get it, but I also thought it was stupid. I'm an artist --- there was a beauty in not remembering parts of your day from drinking. it was a scary, exciting thrill (well, until 4-5 days ago... that's not what I could have ever been hoping for). I'm trying my best right now to rationalize my partial relationship with alcohol without justifying it, because I'm not trying to excuse my blackout... but I never remember deciding I wanted to experience a blackout. again, my tolerance with alcohol is low and I only remember drinking roughly 6 cups of the red wine. I was supposed to and actually planning on going to sleep that night. everything went against the plan. the entire week and now my entire month and actually EVERYTHING got fucking turned on its head.

anyway, so I told them everything cleanly and told them I had nobody with me when drinking, but later she asked me why I'm coming now and didn't call the ambulance. I told them my phone died and she asked "no, but what about someone around you while it was happening?" well I had nobody lol. this entire time, between questions she kept railing me about how I should get a psychologist. she said she doesn't know about my parents as I never mentioned them, only draupner wave and I told her they're abroad. she didn't know anything about me at all. she saw a low point of me and deduced my entire character. she said stuff like "I don't know much about you or your situation other than the stuff you said --- and well, what I can see myself when looking at you... your posture, etc."... well yeah, you're seeing my lumping shoulders, with lazy eyes and apparently a borderline fever and marathon-running heart. good job, you've deduced that I'm a junkie. oh that, too... she asked about drugs. I told them no, she said she "hopes that doesn't become a thing" or something along those lines.

I was very cooperative this entire time. I thought there was no harm in doing any of this, because I had nothing to hide. but my frustration was growing, because I came to the hospital for a medical evaluation. instead, not only did they not inform me of my parameters, but they kept drilling me about getting a psychologist I was anyway already planning on getting. literally the only reason I didn't get one was a fucking practicality. I want to do it and I want to do it right. I want to be fully aware and well-rested when I speak on the phone so I know exactly what I say and what instructions I'm given. holy fuck... it annoys me; when I told her I was anyway already planning to get one, she said "well you should get one now". later she said I probably already have one I chose and I nodded, but she said I need to get it now. she also was struggling to understand how I don't have a family doctor. she told me to announce myself, because it's only 7k a month... she also told the other doctor not to write that this is a drinking-related visit. everything she told me kept going back to "a family doctor". she told me this medicine and that medicine... she told me she couldn't prescribe me this and that she'd write it down on a piece of paper separately (she didn't and I was so overwhelmed I forgot to ask). she drilled me on so much that I started feeling dizzy and light-headed in the chair. I had to lean back, because I couldn't handle her drilling me on about how I'm having a problem that I need to deal with and that I need a psychologist. she didn't stop even then. I don't care that her tone was "caring" and "compassionate", she was not helping anyone in that situation. if anyone knows I have problems (not just practical ones) I have to take care of best, it's me.

she then finally looked into my mouth, said "that's ugly" and deduced it's a viral throat infection... again. two ER visits one month apart and both deduced the same thing. either it came back from the drinking... or it's a general bullshit label they slap onto anything that comes into the hospital that's non-urgent by their standards.

I am disappointed in myself, because I know I'm not suicidal. I know I can reach to that point, but not like this... I have a strong will and if I killed myself, I only ever imagined myself doing it with that will. when I imagined myself dying or killing myself with sorrow and sadness I always got scared, because that's not how I want to go out, not in a million years. having reached to that point in a single night pretty much really really bothered me. but you cannot imagine how happy I am I got out of that hole within a few hours... my will is back. I'll push through this. it still seems infinitely hard to get medical help, but I'll push through it. now especially with knowing my liver is not great, I'm really watching what I eat and am struggling to find things to eat, but I'LL MANAGE.
