protean

📅 Oct 9, 2026

I swore to myself I'd try my best to come up with titles that don't start with the "p", but I just did it again. I'm already having 8 POSTS NEXT TO EACH OTHER SIMPLY BECAUSE OF A LETTER.

anyway. somebody new is charlatan --- or should I call them the gilded traitor or Svengali? let's go with velvettyrant.

I was catching things up with catalyst in September and through them, I found out that velvettyrant became the previously credentialed, now-sunsetted counterparty to catalyst's former closed-membership, two-party interpersonal compact --- the archived incumbent of the retired first-call seat in catalyst's personal network, whose renewal option was not exercised.

fancy words. there's a reason I'm doing this.

I realized this was a great networking opportunity as I had just landed into this town I was once a spectator of. so I texted velvettyrant. I used textbook tactics. I simply asked abt their side and wanted to breach into the circle and it seems I wasn't the only one trying. I had thought about the opening message long enough and I wasn't really planning on acting on it, as I never really do. however, since I moved one thing is different: I need to get this done. I need to move things. fuck--- look at me talk so much.

however, according to velvettyrant (whose words I never decided I could trust), I was the only one that succeeded. I'm quite honestly inclined to believe it. I had no history at all to anything --- or so I thought --- and it benefited me. I wanted to be kind to them, but I didn't want honesty to bite me back, so I kept my distance emotionally. it sucked, because I really want to open up, you know that best. velvettyrant told me everything at the beginning and promptly regretted it. however they said I seemed trustworthy. they were worried catalyst sent me though. I reassured them, I was merely acting out of my own accord. I had no intention of being swallowed in their twisted drama. I didn't want to have skin in the game.

I met with them --- plural. they were the ones to treat me like an outsider. it felt like there was a distance, so naturally my own emotional distance is my only way to stay at an advantage. it's my only weapon, despite me being the one that reached out. I now understand that better. especially now... that I've lost my weapon; more on that later.

but after a certain tabula rasa left, I had a quiet, calmer talk with velvettyrant (as calm as it could really be with these hyperactive, impulsive people). I opened up about redoubt and some other things. I even gave them a gift... the object had no value to me, despite trying to embed it with emotions, so I gave it to them. they valued it far more than me. I feel bitter and grey saying all of this. that day probably made me write boundaries. nothing particularly bad happened. things just... didn't move. I'm not sure what it was. I was struggling to make the day coherent.

a pair of days went by and we met again. and my line with velvettyrant outside of this, as mentioned on the 7th, was/is very thin and can easily snap. or actually that's just the impression that's being pushed on me, by them. nobody needs to tell me this might not be the healthiest person.

I woke up at 14:00 yesterday, just to see they wanted to hang out and about an hour later we already met. I was hoping they'd show me places that could offer some open privacy for my projects (pls don't make it sound weird). instead, we --- well, they --- went on a splurge. this place is small. we met about seven people through several hours. one of them was an instrumentalist and velvettyrant even complained about how they performed on the streets and how annoying or pathetic it was. I simply pointed out it's respectable as it's the artist life and that I was an artist. apparently I'm supposed to be an exception. again, emotional distance was my only key. it was scary.

throughout the day, I stayed hoping that I'd get something out of this --- something practical: locations. I didn't get that. instead, I met with associates of catalyst and was worried about meeting catalyst, before I'd get the chance to tell them about the connection and its extent. the rumours already reached him, but these people didn't know me, so the description was vague. I tried balancing several worlds and several cases: velvettyrant's all-consuming persona, the physical, human links to catalyst and my own clear paranoia. I was trying to juggle between whether or not my typical paranoia was justified or not. velvettyrant didn't help my case. the people we met weren't very fond of them. nobody really was. they have a worrying background, but I cannot tell if it's because of that or because of catalyst. this is such a focused and compressed situation that I don't want to step a foot in. I hadn't realized how concentrated everything was and I managed to pin out the exact center-piece of it. I didn't prepare myself for that.

everybody yesterday made me into a protean. velvettyrant treated me as: a hanger, a camera stand, a lapdog, a servant and the last-standing friend. do I seem proud of my position? redoubt sure wasn't happy to find out about it when I recounted everything.

but then I met with catalyst. I was invited to something. two out of the seven people I had encountered that day were in the city, because of catalyst and this "get-together". velvettyrant told me our "friendship" would cease to operate if catalyst found out, specifically from me. I was having doubts about what the right thing to do was, but when I saw the instrumentalist through the window... I was freaked. I am still so happy I noticed it. this way I got to break the ice ahead of time, I told catalyst I had texted velvettyrant, but I didn't get to say more, because they were ushering me inside. and then... the other person I met was there too. it all played out like in a high-stakes action film --- he immediately snitched me in to catalyst, but he was reassured that I had already told catalyst. it wasn't until about an hour later that through this guy, it got found out that I actually even met with velvettyrant. anyway, I used the circumstancial history of the day to strike up the different conversations and ingrained myself into the temporarily five-member group.

adrenaline was really pumping through me. yesterday was a mess, but catalyst's company treated me well. too well, in fact. I was told this is the bare minimum. I was included. I wasn't made to feel like an outsider. catalyst even told me I'm the longest standing friend they have and they always wanted to reach out, but got forgetful. things I've heard by many other people many other times, except those people I didn't fear or distrust. don't get me wrong, I don't fear catalyst a lot, but I don't trust the type of connections they have in this city. especially considering how the night progressed.

we left and walked around town when suddenly they stopped at two random guys who were smoking and asked for some. I thought they knew each-other they acted like they did. they acted natural --- not only that, but they acted with history. yet it turned out they were strangers and asked for each-other's social media. I pounced and took it too. I have all of their igs. connections. one of these people turned out to live relatively close to me, but more importantly... after hours of talk-n-walk and after already losing two members of the now-six-member group, it turned out one of two people were former classmates of mine from a school I attended for two months, only to leave because of bullying. this person was a friendship-in-the-making back then, but it was pretty superficial and I didn't imagine I'd ever meet them again. it freaked me out. and I told them about my music, about my current project. I told so much, because I thought it'd go into the void and never return. I treated them like strangers. I treated them like some surface-level connections I can make and use up like tokens. I wasn't ready for... this. I'm still not. I don't think I'd ever will be. turned out... the instrumentalist lives a few blocks away from me. that freaked me out too. three of them now know where I live from one single night --- without being drunk as I didn't touch alcohol and only took a few puffs of vape. I gatekept catalyst since I was 15. I never showed them where I lived, only where I lived in the past. I never told them where exactly I was and only met in public places pre-planned. I gatekept them for a reason. talks were nice. cigs were a nice way to distract myself from the issues at the time, but I was always calculated.

in one single night, I got more skin in the game than in my entire life. I didn't want this. they are nice people and I had so much fun with them, which is why I'm worried. I have no idea what their intentions are. I trust velvettyrant far more and there's one strong rationale for it. I was told this "get-together with strangers" is "normal and happens with them daily" and they made me think velvettyrant was poison that I should throw away, just with their actions. they never said anything of such, but they were the quite opposite of velvet and it made me consider things. they are quite literally the type of friendgroup I wanted to be a part of, but I didn't want to have skin in the game and that's the part that scares me.

I know how to handle velvettyrant. it's far easier to understand her, than this. the destiny-level coincidences are also freakish. I understand it's a small town and even velvettyrant reassured me on its (right, they didn't keep their word. I still maintained my connection) normalcy. honestly, seeing how the world around velvet acted... and being from beyond, I'd honestly suggest them to just start fresh elsewhere. it'd really help them see the world differently and not let themselves get trapped with their own genetic malice.

it's 16:07. in about two hours I'm supposed to be meeting up with that same group for some drinks at a bar. I have skin in the game.

throughout my entire day with velvettyrant I barely smiled and honestly that feels more real and more manageable than catalyst.

I never manage to disappoint you, huh... everything always is cranked to the extremes. it's what I live for, despite how powerless and scared it makes me feel. yesterday made me realize I was far more comfortable being nobody in a vast world, instead of somebody in a small--- no, confined one, but I already knew that. I wanted a group in a vast world.

you, please, take care of yourself!
