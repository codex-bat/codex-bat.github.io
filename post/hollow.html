I could barely sleep, because all I kept thinking were the mistakes I've made. I miscalculated so many things. I was terribly disappointed by that, although ik not to blame myself, it was a messy situation to begin with.

I don't want to be seen anymore. not here and not now. well, yes now... but not by these people. I need to start travelling. I plan on being a trainhopper for a tiny while... just for a bit. but idek how to make that happen, even tuglemo got a boyfriend, so I'd assume their place won't be available for me, not as if I had met them yet. but ik that I can count on them... well, 'til now, that is.

well... what a waste of my time. it all feels like a constant struggle. there are no movement with my links. I'm trying to move things. everybody is holding me back. that and the fact that out-of-loop things ceased circulation. I'll go home now. bye.
