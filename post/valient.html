valient

📅 September 25, 2026

I'll be very... vulnerable again --- I have been increasingly, lately. I don't know what it is, but all valorant content fills me with an unexplainable dread. whenever I experience anything related to the valorant trademark, I feel like I might cry...

I have no idea how to justify it. I have no idea how to reason through it. there are so many things at play with these feelings. I feel overstimulated, by my emotions --- by my thoughts. I never planned on ever tributing so much of myself or my feelings towards something so openly, especially not a faceless corporation run by an army...

but it's true. and there are some things related to this:

  • when I first discovered valorant, I was 14-ish. I was lonely. valorant represents my desperate need for connection and my deep, deep... loneliness.
  • I lean on riot games soundtracks a lot, but often I listen to the cinematics on loop instead... hearing the chatter, hearing the agents' actual voices... valorant represents my companions.
  • more than that, I discovered the game during its very beginning --- the beta. the game was born simultaneously with when I was actively imagining my own universe, Opdolpha. it's a coincidence I can never label and that --- to this day --- freaks me out quite a lot. I know it cannot be felt or heard through words alone on a screen, but gosh it's so scary how it all lines up... I grew up with league of legends, so naturally I was already familiar with the visual artstyle and when imagining Opdolpha, I imagined it visually as a Zenless Zone Zero meets League Of Legends with gameplay more keen to WatchDogs X Cyberpunk 2077. during this time (2021), I was actively watching Cyberpunk 2077 walkthroughs and the suicide ending had really gotten to me... it's what inspired Hurtzugen... someone I've placed in Opdolpha, but ended up becoming. v appeared in 2019 in rough shape (Project Ares), but then elaborated itself specifically in 2020-2021 into a very close approximation of my exact idea of Opdolpha's visual, narrative and short-cinematic tone... I literally wanted to expand an idea... a game... an animation, anything to be universal and inclusive to most cultures/languages in a non-harmful way... and v just went ahead and did that --- each cinematic ending with a flashy transition of the official video title in various different languages...in a way, valorant represents Opdolpha and (to me_) my own creative mind behind it.
  • I hate to admit it --- and I might even get rid of this revelation as a fear of being ridiculed... which doesn't stay true to my principles of transparency... for that all I can say is that if this section is gone... you'll always be able to find it in the past and I'll remind you of that in the future as an apology for redacting some things I feel ashamed of. you can find it in the past --- but... I embedded my crushes into Opdolpha... so by proxy, valorant represents... my feelings towards others.
  • I had consumed so many games by that point and they felt too brandy and over-the-top; I wanted a bold tease of emotions in trailers and cinematics and v handed me just that, as I had imagined it for my universe. as I watched more, I felt more and more left behind. during this time, in 2020-2022, I had real family issues with a lot of restrictions becoming more apparent: I had to be home by 3AM, no excuses; I couldn't access my own laptop unless I complied with a lot of preventative rules; my electronics would be confiscated until dinner and after that they'd turn off the wifi overnight... I was useless. I was helpless. my diaries were the only way my ideas could live. the brief windows in which I got my laptop were so short, but I managed to get some concepts made, but... I lacked the skills to do and I didn't get to develop them, because I didn't have time. only in 2022-2023 would I actually get to start making music and that's because I left. valorant represents my fear of being left behind --- no, rather... it represents that I've been lagging behind ever since.
  • v became my outlet. I played the game every now and then, but the social aspect was ridiculously badly managed. I couldn't ever work as a team with others, because nobody'd comply and when I would go out my way, they'd insult me. I often got triggered and got t-banned several times. I stopped playing for months each time I got a several hundred hour ban. but the cinematics never left my eyesight. they carried with me and I have matured and grown as they accompanied my years. whether I like it or not... all of my surpressed emotions bled into them. valorant represents my gradual, but complete isolation...
  • I used to cry to Die For You back then, in 2021... I cried to EGO... just a year ago and most importantly: I cried to the revelations cinematic with visions as the soundtrack... just a year ago. it reminded me of what I don't have... what I can't have. active... fun... friendship. I don't remember the last time I genuinely felt welcomed and good to be around someone. and I can see all of those emotions and feel fragments of all of them whenever I consume them again and again. Wall-E was the only other media that could do that to me, but I feel distant of it. it's from my childhood, which I don't feel connected to at all anymore. I don't feel like it's part of me, even though everybody else says otherwise. and I tried leaving behind v too, but I come back to it... because I'm curious, because I don't want to be left behind by something I feel I nurtured inside me. valorant represents my tears.
  • Opdolpha being nursed in the same spirit as v birthed a rivalry between the two... valorant represents an undefeated and unreachable opponent.
  • I was so deeply fascinated by the art direction behind v's cinematics... I always wondered how the dust particles and other background elements were made. I wondered how some of the laces and pouches worked so flawlessly. valorant quite literally represents my taste in contemporary/modern art.

until the ends of my days... being left behind will be my biggest sorrow. seeing my idea materialized at such accuracy and scale surprised me so much and ended up hurting a lot. I wish --- so hard --- that I made all of this up. and I know this seems entirely subjective and while valorant definitely inspired Opdolpha far more than I might've credited just now, Opdolpha was forming and shaping itself into what it became far sooner than when I found out about v...

I feel... paralyzed, trying to size up to that. I've been trying to study valorant, but I also want to lean away from it, so I can keep my originality --- and I have learnt their UX style pretty well; the directional lines and simple geometric shapes complimenting title cards and such, but regardless of what I do, my emotions burn me out before I can reach anywhere... I get ideas. I do things. I write. I produce. I code... but then it crashes when I witness it all again. and while valorant is the sole contender for my heart's space, meganeko, BOSSFIGHT, DOOM: Eternal and ULTRAKILL all fit to feed this dread and sorrow. I've... never expressed this... this clearly and this plainly... I have no idea what to do with this. I have literally not one person who I could tell this to. nobody I know could hear and even bear the weight of what I'm saying and I never wanted to admit any of this, because... I'm the person who takes life too seriously... I've been labelled that. and literally everybody perceives valorant as just a game with "cool cinematics and cool soundtracks" and it's only natural that it's all meant to make people emotional, it's the whole selling point. marketting doesn't care about your emotions. so admitting any of this is difficult, because my feelings aren't from valorant, it's just what they got bled into. and these feelings are not one-off things either... they're a constant in my life.

what... stop feeling this... it's the only real thing left for me, nothing else feels this way.

— end of line —
