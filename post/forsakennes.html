forsakennes

📅 August 18, 2026

this isn't important.

it all began with my birthday. I was mostly expecting money only. it was nice to receive physical gifts, but they didn't reach my heart. I've learnt that I have become really detached to all of my belongings. I'm used to not truly owning anything. well, now I've spent around ~100+ EUR on fashion... I've been visiting different cropps and ordering some stuff. idk why, but for the first time in a long time, I finally feel the need to express my creativity physically again. back in the west I would add an accessory or two to my outfit, but nothing too bold or big. now, I'm trying to lean in more for that street-techwear mix. I want to get a sewing kit. rn I'm sitting at around 121 EUR and I should keep some for my digital ventures (artistic plugins, etc)... I'm thinking, if I start working anyway, it doesn't matter. I will not reach '<'50 for sure. oh, and I have cash. I still could easily give up on any of these things, but I want to finally... form connections to them. I want to find a way to balance out my need for this with my defensive rejection of it. it wasn't on purpose anyway. I wasn't trying to be the tough guy, it just happened.

you accidentally pressed Space on keyboard, huh? that rarely happens... but thank you. this allows me to be more candid, because I honestly really feel the need to get this off of my chest.

I have been really drowning... you already know that by my VARIOUS other posts talking about drowning from very many situations figuratively choking me. what I mean right now, in this moment is that I really want this streetwear style. I'm wearing basically girl's tennis shoes (I currently have one with a tiny bit of platform) and baggy, AESTHETICALLY TECHWEAR pants with slightly baggy shirts. I wear chains, paint my nails black, wear rings and bracelets. I always carry my slingshot bag/pouch (M.H. style). I barely have any colours on me, mostly shades of grey/black with some colours here and there popping up.

I really wanna go somewhere, somehow and meet a group of ppl who are free. I wanna find punks and just let go of some of my worries about laws and such. I want to leave behind some of me. I want to run around in grass, go into the woods, find old buildings, paint walls --- all without the worries of guidelines and restrictions, but with the assurances of those around me. I always feel so explorative and energetic, but my friends don't share these same impulses and I always feel like I've yet to find my friends. I feel bad for saying that and I'm sorry. I won't name anyone, but they'll know it's about them anyway. and again, I'm sorry...

btw, when I went to that green streetwear store, I looked in the mirror... and despite still being fairly skinny, I was... more toned. I was so happy to see a bit of curves. I haven't been working out, especially now that i don't have my weights with me (they were left in the west), but the several physical activities (including the OH-so-wonderful[<3] swimming) and healthier food definitely helped my desperate little body get better ;3

I, honestly, feel silly for even contemplating any of this: why am I contemplating whether I should spend my money? I'm not spending it on anything valuable. clothes, I have enough. but why shouldn't I spend? I absolutely should! I want to find ppl. I want to belong somewhere. I want to go explore abandoned buildings around and be guided by a group and taught what to look for.

whenever I express my very human needs, I sound so silly to myself. it's much easier and much... more me sounding when I talk with ambition.

"if I don't like how it sounds when I say it outloud, it doesn't sound right. if it doesn't right, I haven't formulated it right. if I haven't formulated it right, I don't know clearly enough what I want, therefore I should either ponder on it until I find a clear statement or give it time." I don't know where it came from, but it did. it's kind of my "validity pipeline" (spoken like a true generative shatbot. what a low fucking bar; anyway~).

I'm so-- no, but I'm massively wasting your time here. this is such a waste of your time. I'm sorry to all of you. this time, you don't see into a fascinating part of my mind or fascinating thoughts of a lumpish state of mind... you're not seeing a grandiose plan unraveling itself in front of your very eyes... you're not even reading the despair and worry in my tone as I share more devastating news about those around me or myself. therefore, I struggle to think of a reason I should make this post other than consistency --- not consistency with writing (because let's be honest, I've been a total wreck with that since I moved), but with who I am and how I act. SERIOSULY WHY ODES EVERYTIHNG I EVER SAY SOUND LIKE IT'S COMING FROM A USELESS FUCKING BOT?!?!? sorry.

nothing clever to see here... just move on from this post (I wish you wouldn't, but that's too desperate to express)
