my first full post in a long time --- how exciting! I'm honestly.. struggling. and--- nothing.

how am I supposed to... continue forward? here's a rundown of things that happened lately: I had my flight on 25th of July (I think?). I stayed in-between two places with redoubt, travelling between them depending on our--- well, no... others' schedules. but now I am finally "settling". redbout had her flight on the 15th of Sept. I currently cut contact with forlorn in an unexpected turn of events (our friendship was[?] the white rabbit, he realized that swiftly). draupner wave left the house yesterday and it just reinforced the emptiness I've been feeling. I have to admit that redoubt leaving did take something away. and I pushed things away too. school is another kind of challenge on its own too (again): my hands shake without my input. I am calm. I can manage my own breathing, but my hands reflect something else. I always had this, but it's never-the-less annoying to see it displayed in public without the ability to control it.

I scouted out a train track to see if I could find a me-spot. I'll continue to do so in planned, but still randomized intervals to find the perfect spots in both time and space. they will call me a vandalist and a houligan, for sure... but I don't plan on touching anything. I just hope to find someplace to run away to. being in this empty house on my own is scary. there are so many blindspots around every corner, it doesn't help with my paranoia.

it's so otherworldly how different the culture is here. bus drivers... just start chatting with you kindly (without any ulterior motives), for the sake of simply chatting: "'good morning'? have you guys just woke up!?" or "have you ever been on that side of town? yeah, you know I don't usually pick people up like this, but I figured I wouldn't let you sit out there at this time of the day." to me were prime examples of what I knew against what the environment around me there presented to me. I feel hurt and broken, being spoken to like this... like an equal, like just a normal human being...

redoubt has gotten busy. they have school. they made a friend. their dad asks for their attention. I got school, but only on some of the days. all teachers in their mannerism are a petty mix between "slacking off" and "this is very hard. keep up". more than ever, I have nobody I can go to. I retreat into my dreams and my laptop. I plan on finding myself a psychologist, but I currently can't even afford it. I just spent $20 on a train-trip to get a package, because a family member I asked was unhelpful and things got heated there too.

I swear I'm not the problem... I'm in a phase of major transition and I rather wish I was alone. I could manage better if I was alone. instead, I have people who are almost there for me, but not quite --- only at arm's reach... it's so scary, it does really make me feel like the problem.

I haven't felt this left-behind and lonely in a while, if ever. I know it was the right thing to do, but I feel so numbed and weak right now. I brought some important stuff with me, but I've yet to properly organize everything. I use a different room to sleep, because it's more comforting instead of my own and I use my actual room's bed as a storage unit. I am going to fix it... tomorrow. but it's so hard to get to it. I'm surprised making food comes with such ease. I expected to be more unable, but I do still have some energy. barely enough. my dizziness hasn't left me. sometimes I feel like I'm going to get into the phase of dizziness that nears passing out, but I never quite do.

no friends around that I feel could help... and this entire post was fueled by one devastating message: playwright writing that she's allegedly going to divulge someone's recent actions to the appropriate law-enforcing agency, because that person allegedly kept playwright 2.0 at their current residency yet again. the other time this happened, playwright 2.0 supposedly begged to come home essentially to playwright.

purportedly, the associate decided he'll live in an apartment and take turns with playw 2, having her sleep over one week at a time (this supposedly failed thus far, rn going into its third week of implementation, because plywrght 2.0 was uncomfortable and asked to go back home) --- good thinking, right? no, where's the consent of both parties? and anyway, as I mentioned pw 2.0 apparently expressed a clear preference to staying with pw instead.

there's also professedly an ongoing sort of lawsuit from the associate's part, claiming that playwright is obstructing his access to pw 2.0 --- which, presumably, isn't true. the associate allegedly got to see pw 2.0 at institutional places and some public, government-owned lands as well, such as city parks. by all means, the associate supposedly is allowed to do anything except go against the best interests of playwright 2.0, which seems to be staying over at playwright (naturally). again, I don't really know enough about this to be able to speak of it, but it does sound muddy to me and these are some unfortunate circumstances.

I am alone, lonely and sad. but you please take care and I hope you're okay <3
