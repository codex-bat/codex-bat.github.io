we own water

📅 Nov 4, 2026

wait, hold on... I just found out about the UK's plan to invest in AI data centers. that's not a great idea.

I appreciate their concerns regarding water usage, but what about gas? I don't mean to sound like a broken record, I'm well aware that everyone is nagging them about that, but let's talk about the government itself, 'ight?

they want to catch up with the evolving industry of AI, so they won't have to rely on overseas servers. I get that, it's a nice sentiment. most conservative people see this as a win — as I've seen many people around me complain about our eastern-european countries' aged Cybersecurity among other things we lag behind in — and it seems the UK is capable of playing catch up. except it's not, is it? if they will use other means of cooling servers, the data centers will have huge carbon emissions and they won't be able to fix that and reduce it. they will fail to meet the set expectations of their legally binding promise from The Climate Change 2008 Act. that's... such a shame. how is greed this corrupting and how come we haven't learnt a thing since before Christ? it's one of the seven deadly sins for a reason. I'm not going to educate you on religion(s), but it goes to show civilizations before us understood very well the mechanisms of mankind and yet we are unable to resist, manage or suppress the impulses and thoughts that lead to the fall of civilization anyway. it's ridiculous. if anything, I might read the Bible with an image of our possible future sci-fi dystopia in mind, as a way to see how much wisdom there was in the past (if we ignore some of the... other things).

and I don't mean to be accusatory, but it's convenient that they're doing this just after having the Bristol City Council's data stolen. oh, but the council wouldn't like to disclose what data was taken — it's ok, just check the dark web. and on top of that, there was just at the beginning of 2026, if I recall from memory: a leak from the age-verification pipeline (discord, right?). on top of that, as of just months ago, they've become the most breached European country — that's an ugly title. and if they aren't willing to do security in-house without overreach and they're not capable of protecting their own society's data, why go for AI instead of expanding security? seriously though — since the government cannot handle Cybersecurity — I think the pollution these data centers will produce is overshadowing the privacy concerns this poses. we know quite well that the UK government is notorious for its hunger to control its people in-and-out. WHY would the people even trust them with their information after the unprofessional, weak security they have — other than they have no choice—? first it was Brexit, which just made moving abroad harder and now they're going to "handle" all of the data better, as if Slough wasn't enough. well, it isn't, because the economic bubble of the US is very intimidating. funny how much the UK is starting to resemble the early Black Mirror seasons, because the government is starting to gain access to the tools that gives them the reach into people's privacy as they desired.

on a far happier note: I read about the midterms from yesterday. I'm very glad to hear about the democratification of the House (amongst other bodies). I've read this means the Trump era is over — is it really? I'm not a democrat and I think there are many republicans who have morally and logically sound values, but most people on any side just seem to argue like dumb monkeys. generally speaking I always noticed they prepare and research only information that benefits them and therefore discussions are always running in parallel. "here's data showing that you suck" and "I hear you. well, here's data showing that you suck." "I hear you as well, well here's data..." — you get the point, but this might as well be an actual transcript. the US also looked like it was heading straight towards dystopia and the end of capitalism, so these elections genuinely made me happy and sigh. I don't mean to say, though, that I think Democrats will turn this around. they're politicians. no politician is good.

anywho, thank you for sticking around for this little rant of mine ^^
