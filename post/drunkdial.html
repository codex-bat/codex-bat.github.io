drunkdial

📅 Oct 19, 2026

I texted forlorn, again — by the title, you can tell it was while I was drunk.

I've realized what I'm doing, but I already knew that and I cannot and won't stop doing it. I'm pushing everyone away, because I don't trust them.

catalyst, never trusted them. quixotic idealist didn't trust them. playwright: I stopped trusting them long ago. tuglemo, didn't trust them. redoubt, I didn't trust them either. velvettyrant, I don't trust them. daupner wave, I don't trust them...

find any one person I've ever talked about... I don't trust them. I trusted forlorn with all of my dark stuff and he never directly broke my trust, but feeling replaced did. it was as if everything I've ever told him and he ever told him slipped into the void. anything I told velvettyrant slipped into the void. anything I say to catalyst slips out of their ordinary life once I'm not around them. I'm nobody's usual. I'm the special friend — the special occasion. I'm nobody.

everything that... happened between me and chemicals slipped into the void. not one thing remained. I trust not one person and not one person wants to be trusted by me, but all of them are offended if I don't. I can't do anything to stop it.

nobody gives a shit that I don't trust. I express it openly, because I hope to be met halfway there. they grieve it as if they couldn't do anything about it. how come I couldn't encounter one person I could trust? you could technically say I trust redoubt, but that wouldn't be true. I trust that she doesn't have ill intensions, because she's harmless. but I struggle to trust her with my emotions.

and it wouldn't have even mattered if it weren't true that forlorn was seeking others, our friendship died and he didn't try to get it back. even if my distrust wouldn't have been there to nurture my fears and doubts, the friendship would've died. I texted him. I saw a song he shared on ig notes and thought it was riddled with bitter irony, but he said he just liked it and it had no subtext. he said he hurts, because of the white rabbit's death, but it happened and he wouldn't have a reason to attack me with a song like that. I didn't even think it was about us, I just made it about him and his way of acting. I was drunk; I wanted to act on my impulses.

I push them allllll away — like a good self-sufficient prophecy. the list from earlier: I could just rename "didn't trust" to "is not my friend" and then I'd have a full list of absolutely zero friends, because that's how many I have — none.

it's pathetic. what does it say of me that I don't even trust my own playwright? or that I don't trust that anything quixotic idealist ever says has substance in it? what kind of monster am I... if I have no friends, how can I claim to be empathic, caring and kind? I have no proof of it... I know I'm trying to protect myself, but I'm hurting and I have not one person to reach out to.

daupner wave keeps telling me I can do whatever I want in the house and that I should relax. she is someone who describes her son as complicated, but ultimately hateful and basically selfish... however just the day after saying that, she talks to him with deceptive sweetness. same thing with me: I heard her mention on a call to someone that there is something I hadn't done right, but that she doesn't want to press me on it to avoid conflicts and that she'd rather be on good terms with me... why am I being managed? still...?! I want honesty — unconditional, non-binding honesty for the purest reasons. I cannot get that... ever.

I didn't want to be a witness, but my hand is forced. someone handed it in, before I could say no. I'm not a witness, currently. anything I've said up until now is basically heresy and should be taken with a huge grain of salt — I was just sharing opinions.

everyday I lose my will to continue everything. so many ideas and projects, but when I sit down to be with them... it all feels so heavy. there's no... point in doing any of that. I dream again — I had a nightmare just this noon. but even that nightmare felt safer than reality... even in the nightmare, I felt freer. I was just in my room with hallucinations. somehow that was somewhat comforting, despite disturbing me. perhaps the fact I could dream again alone comforted me, not the actual substance. I've also dreamt of many other things, most of which I don't want to admit, because I don't want to express my emotions on the subject matter. I'm just glad my passive neuronal activity is back... but I still struggle to sleep sometimes.

I keep myself up a lot. lately I've been flooded with memories. when I kept moving, I forgot most of my experience and life before that place, until 2022 in Romania I remembered my childhood more clearly, but I had begun to forget my early teen years. then, I moved back to the west and I had even forgotten that. now I'm back here and I'm beginning to slowly remember the little details of everything. and it hurts. the fact alone that I compartmentalized based on location bothers me. and it bothers me how miserable everything looks. I wish I could say it's just my current lens washing over the past, but I can't. It sucks; and I have no one to share it with. I never did. I never had anybody to share the pain with and the people I did share it with came with conditions — Ibuprofen, but with conditions and more money and more side-effects.

even if I texted him sober, I can't get him back. I was about to tell him he ended the friendship, but it's just cruelty to remind him of that. but I wanted to express I cannot trust that he's actually hurting from this... but what's the point in saying anything? it's over, that would be just cruelty... but nobody spared me from the pain of cruelty — and therefore, I wrote a post.
