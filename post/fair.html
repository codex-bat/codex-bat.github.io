I'm tired of it.

today's thesis: nobody.... and I mean fucking nobody has my best interests at heart. even if they think they do... nobody considers me.

quixotic idealist, playwright, forlorn... they all told me I had to be the one to take care of redoubt and that I had to protect them. nobody listened when I told them I was running low. I spent two years getting a roughly two-hour trip to her place almost every weekend so we could meet and she rarely could come over, mostly because of the associate (who often even forbade it, because it was "his house"), but often because of her schedule too. she had a leap year and yet still I was the one visiting more.
I brought her to the East for Christmas, so I could show her my past and that pulled at her parents' hearts' strings. now I'm expected to go there again and I'm not willing to. I don't want another plane ride. I said I was fucking terrified of it — I fucking wasn't joking and I'm just picking up the pieces of my life. but Low and Behold — it's "his fault, because he doesn't put as much effort into it and he's an unserious person" — according to redoubt's parents. it's really nice that if we ever have an argument... my family sides with her, but her family sides with her too. I mean, I just can't win. who the fuck is on my side then? sometimes I fight my resentment so much, because I really want to be a good person and I want to love and care without pulling away, but... they're making it really difficult.

and it's not like I'm being an unreasonable piece of shit. I'm bringing up my issues and everyone just tells me to take it easy on her. right... who's going to take it easy on me? and even if nobody will, how am I going to get heard if I can't speak? and I don't entirely blame them, I shouldn't always lash out or bring up issues, because redoubt can't solve them and can't handle or react to them. so then what — am I a dead fish on land?

even her psychologist said she understood why redoubt maternal figure was "upset" and sided with her against redoubt's plans to visit me for Christmas again — now after two years. suggesting that I'm not putting enough effort into the relationship and redoubt just relays this to me with little resistance, because she isn't the type of person to stand up for me and win the argument — or any for that matter. so whatever I do, it always just seems like I'm the motive behind all of her decisions and I'm somehow trying to distance her from her family and life. they make it seem like I'm dragging her down. I'm so tired of being seen this way. they wondered why I never bothered to speak to them when I was there... guess!

there was— is so much wrong there. and when I wasn't keen on inscribing myself, draupner wave was also drilling me about it and name calling me a bunch of stuff. I love being treated like a miserable sack of shit. and when they're nice to me, it's so superficial... all of them (well, except redoubt).

and I understand that playwright's situation is grave, but she also just acts like my cooperation and willingness is just the bare minimum and to be expected, because of the urgency and scale of everything. I cannot quite disagree with that, but it's still chains from my past that I hoped to get rid of and another evidence that they don't have my best interest at heart — only theirs or of those immediately around them. even when I was around, since I didn't say a word it was to be expected that everything was sound. it's so... tragic to feel so unseen. it really bothers me and hurt then. now there isn't even anyone to say anything to, even if I wanted to. so that's fixed. I just literally lurk in the darkness of the house and keep myself functional to walk from one room to another — occasionally exploring the outside world for whatever is needed. help...
