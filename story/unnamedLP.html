“Reality.”

⠀ ⠀ ⠀A beautifully ethereal and seemingly boundless hall was housing a cluster of strange, hovering apparitions. Distant creaks could be heard. The apparitions wore no faces --- instead having tentacular, fleshy threads smoothly covering Their flickering light. Their forms were covered by dark blue robes, lined with grey accents signifying Their various cosmic expertise alongside Their designations. The robes floated as if not bound by any gravity; they merely served as uniforms, to be read and recognized on Eternally Powerful Apparitions. They were waiting, in the absence of one. As They waited, not one sound was heard, except the gentle flow of the water that resided between the paths They hovered over. The hall had several paths organized by a grid-like pattern, but not aimless or excessive --- each path led to another space, unseen by bare photoreceptive organs. The Warp Realm.

⠀ ⠀ ⠀At last, the absent one arrived and the cluster began producing strange, seraphic sounds. They began communicating --- preoccupied with thoughts of The Raze. They were blind to its motives and blind to its power. It didn't communicate its intentions with reality --- it just acted and no soul could figure out why. All any servant of The High Cathedral could do was continue serving, but some of Them cursed moons, believing They were going to lose all of it. They hadn't expected it to appear so soon; some of Them were uncertain of its actuality until it showed. Seemingly never-ending seraphic noise, until one of Them made a ground-shaking sound and the waters moved. That one emitted intimidating, audible violence --- violence prompted by the statements of another apparition. But the ethereal hall shook again. It wasn't Them this time; They couldn't reason through it. They looked around the space as the rumbling subsided --- then complete, pure silence. They were stunned that the movement could reach The Core. They thought it best to slowly depart from Their circle. Once They were far apart from each other, They contorted and shifted into different realms, leaving behind the centralized tunnel-system to fill its emptiness with nothing but fog.
