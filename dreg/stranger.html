stranger

📅 Nov 3, 2026

well, it's decided — I can't sleep. I went to sleep at 1AM, woke up at 4AM... it's 7AM. this is usual by now, no point in rolling around in the bed trying to fall asleep — I won't. and hours later I would probably fall asleep like yesterday from 11 to 15-16. it might be my body's circadian rhythm being messed up, but I can't simply fix it by forcing myself to sleep unless I have some anti-anxiety meds. I plan on buying Valeria herbal pills or whatever to take until I get to see a psychologist.

anyway, yesterday around noon — when I fell asleep — I dreamt of something. there were lots of people and lots of things happened, so I won't be going into the details. it was pretty convoluted. point is, I dreamt of a friend, but sometime later in the dream I was in the past and they couldn't recognize me as their friend as we've never met up until that point. it's a really devastating, bittersweet dream. this time I had an unknown, random imaginary friend who accompanied me to the past, but this isn't the first time I've dreamt of such things and in those dreams... I was alone.

what do these dreams say of me? they're not random. I had this same sort of plot a few times already. I remember about two years ago I had this dream of an ex of mine. I memorized their discord tag and messaged them. I tried to explain everything I knew about them and that we had a relationship, but it was already gone. I had lost the relationship with them and couldn't get it back. they didn't recognize me, but they managed to believe me since I proved I knew a lot about them — she just told me she believed me and that she was happy for that version of her, but that she didn't know me or love me, so we couldn't be together just because of this. "it would be unnatural", I think she said.

this time it wasn't like that. my accompanying partner and I were surprised and curious that "it worked". it was like I was trying to get into this sort of dream and I knew he wasn't real. I was in a sort of lucid state, but it didn't quite play out that way. anyway, I buddy'd up with the guy who couldn't recognize me and I'd start noticing "toys" (don't think of anything sexual please. it just wasn't a very obvious toy. it was a fabricated thing that my mind came up with), random "pens", accessories and such that would later become gifts and memories that I or someone close to him would cherish. it was sad to see him oblivious to them or the value he had at him.

these dreams are bittersweet, because in a way... I feel freedom in them, being in the past, no longer bothered by the present's problems. but losing people, because of it... hurts and bothers me. it feels so tragic (I think that's the exact word I used two years ago) to see them and have them not recognize me. but that loss gives me the vast world of lonesome. I'm in the past with nobody believing me with all of the hardships I have experienced. some of these times I dreamt I had several years of experience. it feels strange to see a timeline change and shift, especially because of my displacement in it.

three hours later and I'm coming back to this, because I'd like to specify that the freedom I feel isn't because I believe the past is set in stone, but rather, because having lost a chunk of the experiences I shared with others, it finalized my already-existing loneliness. it proved all relationships are superficial and that nobody would entertain the idea of something not being "normal", because "that conversation didn't happen" and such — since they couldn't remember something they hadn't experienced yet. the dream with the ex wasn't isolated or confined in a small containment that was the discord chat, I had interactions with others: playwright, for example... didn't remember my ex either and in the waking world I'm pretty sure I had just recently managed to cry to playwright about something, but in my dream I was set back a few months and she hadn't remembered that moment of vulnerability. and I wasn't vulnerable with her in years before that. and I even had a dream where redoubt and I weren't together, but I knew it was a dream and recognized her. I knew we were together and wasn't as worried about it in the dream, but it still felt weird to be a stranger to her. and... recalling my conversation with @forlorn , this strange coldness was exactly what he showed me. it felt like a weird, ethereal connection — like there was no dissonance anymore, just a few dull frequencies, hums... it felt off and sad. I know my waking world and dream worlds are in sync and I treat them as such, because I'm aware of how I am, but that doesn't help. anyway, saying that sounded a bit cocky and arrogant. sry

you shouldn't listen to me and you should go to sleep. sweet dreams 💕
