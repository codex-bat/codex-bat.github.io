hi, welcome back. I deleted the Meta apps long ago as a way to escape the codependency it created. But I'm realizing no matter where you are, one Meta app will always be dominating the field and force your hand into downloading it again mostly because of institutional affairs. School has demanded either I have messenger, WhatsApp, Facebook or a combination of them.

it's obviously become annoying, because I can't escape it until I am institutionalized. but more than that... it always brings me to start profiling the profiles handed to me by the "people you may know" section or whatever. it's become a passive pass-time activity if I'm unable to do anything useful (I'm not going to literally describe scenarios in which I wouldn't be able to like "shitting", but you get the point). it's... weird. I see so many people and so many of them are soo... boring.

there are SO MANY people that have a generic "here's a picture of me or something and a long existential thread about something random or mundane. enjoy misery in the digital landscape." archive and just let it exist. I don't remember the last time I said something like "if you fight, you might lose. if you don't fight, you've already lost." anywhere, at all. it's so pretentious and useless (and above all else, cringe). let it be in a book, I'll accept it in that form — but absolute hard noon social media. it's so goofy.

anyway, and it's also depressing to see how good people's lives are. you might point out how superficial and unrealistic social media is, but if people got the time to get a good, fake pic of themselves, they're doing well. they've got plenty of time. plenty of time I don't usually have, especially not as of lately.
