vigil euthanasia

📅 September 13, 2026

emotions came back... idk how to reason through them.

look at me reasoning through it here anyway... I felt it as I thought it, but writing it requires reasoning it... how can I feel as I write without paper and pen?

I want to kill the rabbit... I'm vengeful. it's been suffering and me along with it. the rabbit doesn't live --- it's simply a concept. all of this time wasted trying to make the rabbit run it again --- I'll take the rock and let it descend with devastation--- but it wasn't wasted time. trying itself developed me further. I became more empathic because of it, no? would I walk all of that back now? but I cannot deny myself all of that frustration. letting go won't let go. it'll fester. this would be symbolic and I know it'll help. but it's cruel to it.

I should stay and sit down besides the rabbit. I should nourish it as it dies and provide it with comfort. I should pet the rabbit. I want to give it cuddles or at the very least breath with such tenderness that it feels safety in death, itself. this is who I am. animals love me for this, so does this rabbit. I care.

this is exhausting. what about me? I can't just let go of all of that frustration.

how I act is dictated by everything leading up to it. what this rabbit made me feel... it nurses my choice and me into acting. kill the rabbit.

I've tried reasoning with the rabbit. I tried telling it not to go there. I tried holding the rabbit back from going there. I knew the rabbit wanted freedom, but I also knew the rabbit didn't want this. I wouldn't ever say this with such certainty, but I sincerely and genuinely knew the rabbit didn't want this, not even as an act of defiance. it was simply clueless...

it's all the vet's fault. I called them. they were supposed to dispatch a team, it was "en-route" actually... but they chose another case instead. it's their fault.

no, they couldn't have known. how would they know which case was in need?

they knew. they deemed this one unworthy of saving. left it to die. now I'm the one left to deal with its death, whether I comfort it, leave it up to nature or engage in its demise.

did you tell them on the phone what state the rabbit was in?

BUT OF COURSE. I TOLD THEM EVERYTHING. OVER AND OVER AGAIN. I reasoned with them. they avoided and denied it. they sought after their own comfort and ease.

they just wanted to do their job right.

we're just speculating... I'm just speculating. regardless... this rabbit is dying. what do I do to my poor rabbit? it's not the rabbit's fault, actually. it's the vets'... but do I hold it against them? it's the right thing to do, but what good comes of it?

"what good comes of it"? THAT I DON'T GET PULLED INTO THIS SITUATION AGAIN. instead of relying on the vets', I make sure next time I take care of it myself, instead of having the rabbit's inevitable death crush me. I make sure I won't have anything to do with this rabbit again... the vets' have become resentful towards my calls. they gave up on my rabbit's situation long ago (yes, this has been on-going. the rabbit had many sicknesses, all stemming from this... but alas, this rabbit is laying on my grass, breathing its last breaths).

it's intimate.

you cannot simply identify this as "cruel" or "an act of mercy", "emotional"... it's so much more complex than that. there are so many variables. the rabbit's needs... my needs... it'll be me who deals with the consequences of the rabbit, but it's the rabbit dying. can the rabbit still think reasonably and critically to know what it wants in its final moments? emotional signals are important, but what if they completely overwrite how the rabbit usually acts and is? does it even actually matter...? the rabbit will be dead, it won't be wrestling with its identity in its death... but what if it recognizes this contradiction in character and dies with regret in its last miliseconds of thoughts?

do I know best? if I was detached from the situation would that be any better? be it me, smashing the rabbit with the stone without flinching or caring in the middle of a forrest... is that any better?

do I KILL the rabbit?!? I think I might kill the rabbit. the rabbit is dying.

important clarification... now that I'm out of my thoughts: this all stemmed from what I put in my discord bio:

  • you know what? I've decided that if something's dying, I'll be the one to end it.
  • actually, how can you regulate between vengeance, grief, mercy and inaction (letting nature go through its course)?

there are no living beings being (hah, being being) threatened here and I'm not commiting murder. the rabbit is a meta-physical concept.
