glorious

📅 Nov 27, 2026

I am very anti-corp. we are all aware of that, but funnily enough there is one company I have pledged my loyalty to without realizing it.

the first time I got their products, I was genuinely so happy. I was just 14, nearing 15 and for my birthday I asked for one thing: the Glorious Model O wired mouse — one that was functional until just a year ago (and one that is still semi-functional, but with buggy input). Last year it stopped working right and just started causing frustration as I tried working. I was furious, but part of me was sad to let it go. it was a very specific mouse and I got it for a very specific reason. I specifically got the Black matte one, because I wanted the mouse's texture so I could drag click on Hyp: Bedwars. I was watching this one youtuber that I can't name, he had a reddish skin. it wasn't one of the big ones, but he had around 300k subs in 2020 or so? I loved his videos, but I think he got carpal tunnel or surgery and wanted to delete his channel or something along those lines... well, no matter! I really loved that guy. I decided I wanted his mouse and got the model O. it was the first birthday when I felt like I was choosing for myself. then, when the wired one stopped serving me well last year, redoubt got her parents to get me the same one, but wireless for Christmas. sadly that one stopped sooner, as the battery is no longer charging as of a month ago or two. but it's alright, redoubt decided she wanted to get me yet another glorious mouse. this one is wired again and it's glossy white model D-. but I've found exact tutorials on how to swap the batteries of my wireless model O, so I'll have three functional glorious mice, with one being a bit more... rested.

so that's the one brand that I have had follow me for years and has been by my side. even as I stopped playing competitive games (specifically bedwars) and started being more productive, these mice still are my comfort zone somehow.

some day I'll get one of their keyboards, one day when I will be able to afford it.

when I was 14, these mice were the top tier; they were novelty to me. I didn't care about Logitech or Razor — I wanted Glorious. and I still do. I'll check out the company, but I don't want my loyalty misplaced.
